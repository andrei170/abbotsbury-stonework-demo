Four ways in

From a finished mason to someone who wants to learn the trade.

Qualified stonemason

You know stone

  • Walling, cladding, pointing, restoration
  • Level 2 or 3 qualified
  • Can run a section of a job
Drystone waller

Walls without mortar

  • Field and boundary walls
  • Rebuilds and new walls
  • An eye for coursing
Improver / labourer

On your way up

  • Some time on stone sites
  • Wants to progress
  • Reliable, physical, tidy
Apprentice

Learn the trade

  • No experience needed
  • Learn alongside qualified masons
  • A trade for life
